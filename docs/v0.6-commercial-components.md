# v0.6 Commercial Quote Component Contract

This document defines the additive commercial-component model for v0.6. It preserves the existing `price` field and rfqdiff compatibility while making real supplier quotations comparable.

## Design principles

- Never silently treat conditional, optional, excluded, or unknown charges as included.
- Preserve the supplier's stated currency and amount before FX normalization.
- Derive totals only from explicit numeric components.
- Record reconciliation differences instead of overwriting supplier totals.
- Keep commercial normalization additive; the v0.5 `price` contract remains valid.

## Component model

Each component contains:

- `type`: equipment, freight, packing, origin_charge, destination_charge, tax, surcharge, discount, other.
- `description`: source-faithful short description.
- `status`: included, excluded, optional, conditional, unknown.
- `currency`: ISO 4217 code when monetary.
- `unit_price`: supplier-stated unit price.
- `quantity`: multiplier when explicitly stated.
- `supplier_total`: supplier-stated component total when present.
- `computed_total`: `unit_price * quantity` when both are known.
- `normalized_total`: converted amount in portfolio currency when the component participates in the comparable total.
- `condition`: free text for triggers such as detention after free time or border charges at actual cost.

## Comparable total

`comparable_pre_tax_total` is the sum of normalized components whose status is `included`.

Excluded, optional, conditional, and unknown components are reported separately and never silently added.

## Reconciliation

When `unit_price`, `quantity`, and `supplier_total` are all present, the normalizer records:

- `computed_total`
- `supplier_total`
- `difference`
- `matches`

This catches real RFQ ambiguities such as a freight quote stated as EUR 3,900 per truck for two trucks, where the confirmed total is EUR 7,800.

## Anonymized regression scenarios

The v0.6 test suite should cover:

1. Per-unit freight multiplied by explicit quantity.
2. Ocean freight plus destination and inland charges.
3. Conditional border/detention/cancellation charges excluded from comparable total.
4. Flat-rate service/overhaul with lead time and payment terms unresolved.
5. Mixed-currency commercial components normalized under one pinned FX policy.

No supplier names, email addresses, customer names, shipment addresses, or other identifying correspondence data should be committed as fixtures.
